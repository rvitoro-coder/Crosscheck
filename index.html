INTERLET™

Daily puzzle
Find the hidden 5-letter word in 8 guesses. After each guess, LETTERS tells you how many letters match. PLACED tells you how many are in the right spot.
How to play

HOW TO PLAY

Guess the hidden 5-letter word. Every guess gives you two clues.

LETTERS

How many letters from your guess appear anywhere in the answer.

PLACED

How many of those letters are already in the correct position.

EXAMPLE · HIDDEN WORD: BEACH
CRANE→31

C, A and E are in BEACH. A is already in the right spot.

The game does not tell you which letters matched. Working that out is the puzzle.

Repeated letters: If you guess the same letter twice, it only scores twice if the answer has it twice.
